Privacy Policy — Pip's Penguin Rush

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Pip's Penguin Rush does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account is needed and no sign-in is offered.
  • There are no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • Your progress (scores, fish, unlocked penguins, missions) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
  • The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Questions? Contact jeroen@vandijk.app.
