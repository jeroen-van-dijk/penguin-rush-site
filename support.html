Support — Pip's Penguin Rush

How do I play? Swipe left/right to change lanes, up to jump, down to belly-slide. Double-tap (or tap the SLED button) to ride a Lucky Sled that survives one crash.

How do I get a higher score multiplier? Complete all three missions shown on the menu. The multiplier goes up after that run.

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the app resets your progress.

Found a bug or have an idea? Email jeroen@vandijk.app.
